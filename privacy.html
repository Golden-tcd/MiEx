MiEx Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

MiEx is a personal expense tracker built by Golden_TCD. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we don't collect it.

What MiEx stores

When you use MiEx you may enter expenses (name, amount, category, date), a monthly budget, your first name or nickname, and your preferences (currency, light/dark mode). This information is saved only in your own device's browser storage (local storage).

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not send your expenses, budget or name to any server.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

Internet use

MiEx downloads its own files (the app, fonts and charts) from the website that hosts it, and keeps a copy on your device so it can work offline. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request). We do not access or use those logs to identify you.

Backups and exports

If you choose "Backup" or "Export CSV" in Settings, MiEx creates a file on your device. You decide where that file goes. If you share it with another app, that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

Your control

  • Open Settings → Delete all my data to erase everything MiEx has saved.
  • Clearing the site's data in your browser settings, or uninstalling the app, also removes it.
  • Because the data lives only on your device, clearing it or losing the device without a backup means it cannot be recovered. Use Backup regularly.

Children

MiEx does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If MiEx ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
